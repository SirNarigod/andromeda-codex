# ARPG Stage 04 — Resources, Cooldowns and Consumables V0.5.0

This stage adds an adaptive resource/cooldown authority without forcing a traditional class resource. CharacterCore remains authoritative for health and the numeric PRIMARY pool. Stage04 owns action costs, cooldown ledgers, action interruption locks and test-only placeholder consumables.

The three consumables in this stage are **not Andrômeda canon items**. They exist only to validate the gameplay contract before Stage05 itemization binds real content.

The roadmap is revised to add Stage09 `GATHERING_WORK`: mining, wood, flora/resources, extraction and professions for both player and NPC workers. Master resource zones, profession data and production chains are read-only sources. Flora distribution is currently blocked in the Master and must not be spatialized silently.
